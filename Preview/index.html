What each hook does on screen

  • Album colored Now Playing pulls the dominant color off the artwork and flows it through the background, the knob, the bottom bar, and the tab tint. Different tracks change the palette live.
  • Apple Music lockscreen pushes the artwork to MPNowPlayingInfoCenter at 3000x3000 with the com.apple.Music service identifier. iOS gives it the big-art lockscreen treatment, same as the stock Music app.
  • In-app settings opens over Spotify on a long press of the mini bar. Writes land in both sandbox defaults and CFPreferences, so a hook from either path reads the same keys.
  • Premium flags return true for ads, skip limit, scrubbing, extreme quality and Spotify Connect. Fisher Yates replaces the weighted shuffle.
